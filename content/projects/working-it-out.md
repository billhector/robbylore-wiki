# Working It Out (Mike Birbiglia)

Comedy podcast hosted by **Mike Birbiglia**, built around comics working out unfinished material on tape. [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s episode — titled **"It's a New Dawn"** (released **June 2026**) — was billed as their first in-depth conversation, *"two cats cautiously getting to know each other."*

## Key Takeaways

- **Fullest [[career/wga-strike-speech|WGA-speech]] retelling yet.** Robby reconstructs reading the union's financial statements as a former accountant, flagging the head's **$2.1M salary**, arguing the guild should *"be a revolving door in the courts"* instead of striking, getting booed by ~3,000 peers, and being told to *"sit down."* Adds: the union's own employees are now **suing the union**. Friend **Carmen Christopher** walked her out. This is the speech [[people/john-mulaney|Mulaney]] witnessed → directed [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]].
- **"Marriage is a hack."** Robby's strongest articulation of her [[themes/marriage|marriage]] thesis: *"You don't choose your family — but with marriage, you do. One time, you get to pick your family."* Gabby is the family she chose.
- **Marriage cross-teaching:** Robby (raised in inescapable [[themes/family|sibling conflict]]) taught [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby]] that conflict isn't dangerous; Gabby taught Robby to *"care less what other people think."*
- **[[projects/hacks|Hacks]] casting:** they wrote the Randi part for her, then *"made me audition for it"* — which she found more humiliating than a cold read.
- **Crown Heights / Lubavitch detail:** born in Crown Heights (not Queens), into the **Lubavitch** community; notes **Chabad** as a more outreach-oriented, "relaxed" branch. The **kippah** is *"to remind you of ego — that there's always something bigger than you."*
- **New material workshopped on tape:** the *"[[bits/ai-is-autistic|AI is autistic]]"* premise (Elon Musk + Sam Altman as the "most autistic" architects), the *"[[bits/bros-before-hoes|bros before hoes]]"* joke name, and the they/them stance — *"it's annoying and we'll do it."*
- **Will Smith / Ellen / "good guy" critique** — Robby argues she's never *"purported to be the nicest person alive,"* so nobody emails her demanding she be; *"if you see me, do not say hello."*
- **"Not Too Far 1K"** — her [[projects/too-far|Too Far]] charity run that *"gives money to Mexicans"* (no IDs checked) as ICE commentary.
