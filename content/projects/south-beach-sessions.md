# South Beach Sessions with Dan Le Batard

Long-form, confessional interview show hosted by sports-media personality **Dan Le Batard**. [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s episode (released the week the [[projects/hacks|Hacks]] series finale aired, **May 2026**) is one of the deepest career-and-psychology interviews she has given — Le Batard probes anxiety, money trauma, self-parenting, and the [[people/john-mulaney|Mulaney]] origin story.

## Key Takeaways

- **Anxiety vs depression breakthrough.** Robby's baseline is "comfortable nervous," not depressed. An antidepressant once *"made me crazier"* because it lifts people who are already up — she needed a "downer." Frames a nightly glass of wine as a deliberate down-regulator. See [[people/robby-hoffman]] § Neurotype.
- **"The calling is a nightmare."** Doing your calling while poor has *"no guarantee of payoff"* — she keeps a day job long past the point most would quit, framing accounting as her self-built "plan B."
- **"I'm a PhD in me."** The self-parenting / self-love thesis: with nothing else to rely on growing up, Robby invested in the one asset she had — herself. *"Why would I mistreat me? I'm a paying customer… I'm the one who showed up."*
- **The [[people/john-mulaney|John Mulaney]] text, read verbatim** — Robby pulls up the original message on her phone (*"Hi Robby, this is John Mulaney. Dan Levy gave me your number…"*). The most complete version of the [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]] origin on record. See [[people/john-mulaney]].
- **Ted Sarandos meeting** — she met the Netflix co-CEO while grazing the craft-services table at [[projects/everybodys-live|Everybody's Live]]; he introduced himself simply as *"Ted,"* and she blanked.
- **Adam McKay** once proposed writing together; Robby *"backed out of the room"* — her example of fear she didn't overcome.
- **Money as health.** Extends her [[themes/money|money]] thesis: capitalism is *"traumatizing,"* first-class-vs-coach is *"apartheid,"* and having money is *"way better than I thought."*
- Plugs across the episode: [[projects/hacks|Hacks]] + [[projects/the-rooster|Rooster]] on HBO, [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]] on Netflix, [[projects/too-far|Too Far]] on Patreon, robbyhoffman.com tour dates.
