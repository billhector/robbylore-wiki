# I'm Nervous

Robby Hoffman's **first stand-up comedy special** (2019), **recorded at the Just For Laughs comedy festival in Toronto for Crave TV** (per Wikipedia / Forbes 2019). Same year as her [[projects/odd-squad|Odd Squad]] Daytime Emmy win.

## Key Takeaways

- **Format:** one-hour stand-up special.
- **Recorded at:** Just For Laughs Toronto, 2019.
- **Distribution:** Crave TV (Bell Media's Canadian streaming service).
- **2026 Juno Award nomination — Comedy Album of the Year** (announced 27 January 2026 per CBC Music). The ceremony takes place at Hamilton's TD Coliseum on **March 29, 2026**, hosted by **Mae Martin**. Co-nominees in the category:
  - *Dragonflies* — Adam Christie
  - *Fish From the Jar* — Charlie Demers
  - *Dawud* — Dave Merheje
  - *Homesick.* — Faris Hytiaa
  - *I'm Nervous* — Robby Hoffman
- Robby on GQ × Mulaney: *"That was in Canada. Doesn't even count. It's like having a special in Brazil. Who cares?"* — i.e., she does not consider it equivalent to [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]]. The Juno nomination is, in retrospect, a complication of that dismissal: the Canadian comedy establishment was paying attention.
- Bundled with the 2019 Daytime Emmy as the **breakthrough year** before her US-side career took off.

### Commission terms (We Got Company, Sep 2026)

Robby gives the numbers and the constraints for the first time:

- **The fee was CAD $25,000** — and she is unsentimental about the conversion: *"It's going to be 25,000 Canadian. It's about $6 here. I'll take it."*
- **Her manager at the time advised waiting** for a better offer. She refused, in a line that doubles as a money principle: *"I don't wait for anything. A dollar today is worth more than a dollar tomorrow."* See [[themes/money]].
- **Six weeks to prepare.**
- **She had never performed a full hour.** Up to that point she was only getting 15-minute spots, so she assembled the hour from chunks — *"I thought, this will go here, then we start off, then it goes right into here"* — and **the first complete hour she ever did was the one being taped.**
- Her stated method for surviving it is the one she applies everywhere: *"one theme in my life is not thinking too much… It's a brutal day, but then I just have to go and do it."*
- **No creative control over the venue**, and she frames that as normal for her level: *"I have no choice in anything. I'm not at your level where I get to go here and there."*
- The special is also the origin of the [[bits/zero-personality-disorder|zero personality disorder]] bit, which she still uses.
