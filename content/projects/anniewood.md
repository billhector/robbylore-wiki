# Anniewood Podcast

**Anniewood** is [[people/annie-lederman|Annie Lederman]]'s podcast, recorded out of **Bad Ladder Studio** and produced by Annie's twin brother **Todd** (Walker Media). It is the show [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] has returned to **more than any other** — three appearances across two years — which makes it the closest thing in the wiki to a longitudinal record of her life.

Robby's running joke about the venue: *"the cheapest studio in Hollywood,"* *"run by drug addicts."*

## Key Takeaways

- **Three Robby appearances**, and each lands at a different stage of her life — the arc is the reason this page exists.
- **Ep. 20** (Mar 2023) — pre-Gabby, pre-fame, recorded in **Ybor City, Tampa**. The top-surgery episode.
- **Ep. 89** (Jul 2024) — "Buried Alive." The deepest family-history dig in the source set.
- **Ep. 127** (Apr 2025) — "Robby Hoffman has HUGE NEWS." Post-wedding, post-Comedy-Store, mid-press-surge.
- **Annie was at the wedding.** She attended Robby + [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby]]'s [[career/vegas-elopement|Vegas elopement]] (Jan 11, 2025) — confirmed on Ep. 127.
- **The recurring format** is Robby and Annie ganging up on Todd, plus Robby's standing takedown of Annie's **Tesla** (against her own 1983 Toyota Corolla): *"Steve Jobs would roll over if he saw that you just tapped an iPad onto the dash and it wasn't integrated."*
- **A running unfulfilled bit:** a Marie Kondo session on Annie's cluttered, reptile-filled apartment, repeatedly planned as Patreon content. Robby's verdict on the Labradoodle's living conditions: *"abuse for the dog."*

## The three appearances

### Ep. 20 — "Is Top Surgery Right for You?" (March 2023)

The **pre-Gabby** episode, and the primary source for [[career/top-surgery|Robby's top-surgery decision logic]]. Her reasoning, given in full here, is the cleanest statement of the [[bits/two-year-decision-rule|two-year rule]] in practice:

> *"Either way you have regret. The only thing I knew was when I had them it wasn't great. So once I cut them off and don't like it, I end up in the same place — I have a chance of liking it, something I don't know."*

Also from this episode: 3% breast tissue left intentionally; a roughly three-week recovery before her first show back; the on-camera reveal with Annie matching in a sports bra. Plus her mother **Connie** mailing her six Cosby records and three CDs after an HMV sale (years after the scandal — Connie hadn't heard), and the brother *"Shanaer"* Pride-bulletin call. See [[themes/family]].

### Ep. 89 — "Buried Alive" (July 2024)

The densest family episode anywhere in the sources. Net-new material that appears nowhere else:

- Her brother [[people/schmuly-hoffman|Schmuly]]'s **shop-class wooden dildo**, which their mother still displays on her piano.
- **Big Sister "Sherry"** and her male roommate "Stu" — and the mother shutting down the Big Brother/Big Sister program entirely after the brother's match suggested an overnight: *"He's not going anywhere; you'll grow up without a father from here on out."*
- Her father's line about Robby being gay: *"It's promiscuous behavior not befitting a daughter of Israel."* See [[themes/queerness]].
- The mother's **AIDS-needles-in-cinema-seats** paranoia, and the trained seat-patting that went with it — the origin of the [[bits/aids-popsicle|AIDS popsicle]] cluster. See [[themes/orthodox-upbringing]].
- Her *"if I buy a sweater, I get rid of a sweater"* declutter rule, traced to a shopping lesson at 14 or 15.

Annie was turning 41 the week it dropped.

### Ep. 127 — "Robby Hoffman has HUGE NEWS" (April 2025)

The status-change episode. Robby had just been **passed at the Comedy Store** (see [[career/comedy-store-passed]]), had eloped in January, and was fielding TMZ-level attention. Annie's framing: *"Robby Hoffman, fresh from the gyno, the blowing up, the new television star… her wedding in Vegas we attended."*

Robby's own opener — *"fresh from the gyno"* — plus the *Love on the Spectrum* reference she credits to Gabby (*"she feels like she understands me better with each episode"*). Also the fullest confirmation that [[projects/dying-for-sex|Dying for Sex]] had Robby playing a sexual guide to Michelle Williams' character.

## Why it matters

Most shows in this wiki got Robby once, on a press tour, saying press-tour things. Anniewood got her three times across a stretch where she went from a pre-surgery comedian in a Tampa studio to a married Netflix headliner — with the same host, the same rapport, and no publicist steering. The 2023 episode is the only substantial **pre-Gabby, pre-fame** long-form Robby interview in the source set, which makes it disproportionately useful for anything about who she was before the [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]] era.
