# The Interview (The New York Times)

**The Interview** is the *New York Times*' weekly audio-and-video podcast, co-hosted by **Lulu Garcia-Navarro** and **David Marchese**. Each week one of the hosts sits down with a "fascinating and influential" cultural, political, business, or sports figure. [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby Hoffman]]'s episode — *"Growing Up Poor Never Leaves You"* — was published **June 27, 2026** and interviewed by Garcia-Navarro at Robby's LA home.

## Key Takeaways

- **Two-sitting format.** The show talks to each guest twice; Garcia-Navarro spoke with Robby a second time "a few days later" for the fame/marriage portion. The episode is stitched from both.
- **Filmed in Robby's home.** Robby's running gag: the show says it films at the guest's house "so the person is comfortable," then rearranges the whole house — *"the couch isn't where the couch… you want me to be comfortable but the whole house is moved."*
- **Comfort-is-a-rich-concept thesis.** When asked if she was comfortable, Robby kept saying no — and turned it into the episode's spine: *"the idea of comfort generally… is a rich thing."* See [[themes/money]].
- **Class as the organizing frame.** Garcia-Navarro's closing read: Robby puts the poverty and trauma America hides "front and center." Robby's answer: *"classism affects everybody… it shouldn't be a new conversation. It's the conversation."*
- Covered: rich-weird-vs-poor-weird, her father's abuse of her mother, becoming secular, being [[themes/queerness|outed at 17]], *"comedy chooses you,"* the pitbull/celiac backlash, the [[projects/hacks|Hacks]] Yiddish-line criticism, and [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby]].
