# Death and the Afterlife

When asked what happens after death, [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] gives the same argument almost word for word in two interviews recorded within weeks of each other in autumn 2026 ([[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]] and [[projects/rip-jordan-jensen|RIP Jordan Jensen]] Ep. 75). It is the closest thing on record to a worked-out metaphysics. She is post-Orthodox and doesn't practice (see [[themes/orthodox-upbringing]]), but she isn't an atheist either.

## Key Takeaways

- **"Nothing has ever been nothing."** Her central claim: we have no experience of nothingness — *"even a black hole hasn't been nothing"* — so oblivion is the most foreign outcome, not the default. An afterlife *"makes as much sense as a life. None of this makes sense."*
- **"There's nothing I don't believe in."** It's as strange that we're alive *"and I'm not wearing batteries or plugged in"* as that anything would come after.
- **God as a monolith, not a manager.** She leans toward one God over many, *"not that it's an interactive god, but that everything is connected… everything is everything and nothing is nothing."* Bottom line: *"I know nothing."*
- **The consolation case.** If consciousness simply stops, there's no experience of it either: *"there will be no fear… no 'wait, I want to be with my wife.'"*
- **The meaning of life is to live one.** *"I think the point of life is to live a life. That's it."* Wanting a higher purpose is *"a little selfish… You're at the dance. Dance."* (You Made It Weird.)
- **It takes a life to know a life.** *"If God willing I get 120 years… I will have needed every day of every year to figure this out."*

## Details

### The project car

Her metaphor for a life: *"You get one little project car"* and it's your job to keep the transmission, motor, wipers and paint in order. *"You're born with a dependent and it's yourself first and foremost."* It's the same self-as-dependent idea she gave her younger sister, now framed as a duty from God or *"forces, whatever"*: *"Can I count on you for this one?… Please, I can't be micromanaging this one. I have a lot to do."* Compare the *"weird car"* line on [[projects/we-got-company]] and the self-parenting thesis on [[bits/lil-rob]].

### Fear of it

On RIP Jordan Jensen she says death has terrified her since childhood, and she ties that to the OCD habit of dreading everything to come. The fear hasn't gone away. She is *"horrified"* by the thought of [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby]] dying. Her grandfather's deathbed advice, that she was *"living her life now,"* is one reason she puts life ahead of career.

### Ghosts

She believes in ghosts but has never seen one, and she doesn't see why a dead relative would bother scaring anyone. Her teenage reply to a friend worried her grandmother might haunt the house: *"Why would she want to scare you? She was the sweetest lady."* Her own plan as a ghost: *"I'm not going to your house"* — she'd wait for you to come.

### Surviving Death

Robby recommends Netflix's six-part documentary *Surviving Death* (about eight years old). She praises it as *"a clinical look"* at near-death experiences and reincarnation claims, with neurologists and scientists applying the scientific method. She retells its case of a three-year-old who named a wife and a place in Russia that turned out to exist. She doesn't say she's convinced.
