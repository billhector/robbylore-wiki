# Family

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby Hoffman]] is **the seventh of ten children**, with six older and three younger siblings. She was raised largely by her mother, **Constance**, after the family fled her father in Florida for Montreal. Robby has had **no relationship with her father since her early 20s**. She is married to [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby Windey]] and has **no children**. Family is the structural fact under almost every other story she tells: religion, money, comedy, even her marriage.

## Key Takeaways

- **Ten kids; Robby is #7** (Guardian, May 2026). Her mother had all ten by age 30, born about 13 months apart.
- **Mother:** Constance, born in Montreal. She joined the Lubavitch movement in the 1970s, later became a born-again Christian, and raised the ten kids largely alone. Robby says her mother is autistic.
- **Father:** American, charismatic, alcoholic and abusive, mostly absent. Estranged since Robby's early 20s. By Robby's 2026 accounts he is living ("somewhere in Nevada").
- **The escape:** her grandfather [[people/nathan-letovsky|Nathan Letovsky]] pulled the family out of Florida, and [[people/uncle-eddie|great-uncle Eddie]] took all eleven of them in, in Montreal.
- **Siblings named in her interviews** include [[people/kaya-hoffman|Kaya]] (oldest), [[people/schmuly-hoffman|Schmuly]], Menachem, D'vorah and [[people/yehudis-hoffman|Yehudis]] (now her assistant). See [Siblings](#siblings).
- **No kids:** *"I'm not having kids. This is what I do"* (South Beach Sessions, May 2026).

## Parents

### Mother (Constance)

- **Name:** Constance ("Connie"). Robby wished her a happy birthday on-air using the full name: *"Her name is Constance. Happy birthday, Constance."* (Tea Time, Jun 2025).
- **Background:** born in Montreal, became Lubavitch as a young adult in the 1970s, and later became a born-again Christian. She fled the father in Florida with the kids. Robby's summary of the arc (Senses Working Overtime, 2024): *"My mother grew up with a very very close relationship with her father, then she married my father so she lived under a man… then they divorced and then her father died and now she found Jesus so she has a new Daddy and she loves it."* See [[themes/orthodox-upbringing]].
- **Autistic, and a "seeker"** (Here To Make Friends, Sep 2026). Robby says it directly, and cites her mother's own answer when asked what made her vulnerable to religious recruitment: *"Well, if you're asking if I had any friends, the answer is no."* A loner who *"likes books and music"* in a decade when *"everybody was getting into something."* Robby: *"if you are at the end of an escalator with a pamphlet, she will take it,"* and *"I'm like my mother in many ways."*
- **The daily routine:** up at **5:30am** every day to cook, clean and care for the kids. *"Emotionally absent"* but *"definitely physically present"* (Guardian, May 2026).
- **Twelve years of night school.** Per Endless Honeymoon (Oct 2025), the grandfather babysat one evening a week so she could take a night class at Concordia. She took **one class per semester for 12 years** and graduated around the time Robby finished her own three-year Montreal undergrad. *"She just by example was so tenacious."* On NPR's Newsmakers (Sep 2026) a sibling's summary: the *"definition of putting one foot in front of the other."* Robby's frame is that stability and a present mother were the load-bearing parenting; she **didn't need or get curation.**
- **Off the grid:** "a crazy reader" who doesn't own a cell phone or follow pop culture (Anniewood Ep. 20, Mar 2023). She once mailed Robby six Bill Cosby records and three CDs because they were on sale at HMV, years after the scandal, which she hadn't heard about.
- **Lessons and fears:** she taught Robby that it's [[themes/money|"important to give, to remind yourself you're in a position to give"]]; had a *"reverence for doctors"*; and carried AIDS-era paranoia into family life (see [[bits/aids-popsicle]]).
- **Where she lives now** (Here To Make Friends): in Canada with Robby's brother, a **single father**, and his **six-year-old daughter**. Three people share **one bathroom**, and the mother and niece squabble over mirror time. Robby offered the down payment on a three-bedroom, two-bath house if they'd carry the mortgage. Her mother rejected it **over the bus route**: she needs to board early enough to get a seat and read on the way to the library. *"So that's not good enough."*
- **On being material** (We Got Company, Sep 2026): *"you never tell them I tried, you never tell them I did the best I could."* Robby: *"Ma, that's not the joke."* Then, for the record: *"that's the best she could. I wouldn't tell anyone that. It wasn't great."* She is also *"extremely proud"* and lets Robby use whatever she needs.

### Father

- **Who he was:** American, *"very charismatic and very popular and cool,"* charming, alcoholic, religious and abusive. He had a car, which is how Robby's mother tells the origin story: *"and he liked me"* (Here To Make Friends). He was mostly absent, and once left young Robby at a gas station after a candy stop. He had briefly worked for her grandfather in Florida.
- **The divorce:** the split and the move to Montreal happened when Robby was "between the ages of five and 11"; she is hazy on the exact timing. See [the escape](#growing-up).
- **The Porsche:** instead of paying child support after the split, **he bought himself a used Porsche** for about $10,000 while the family was on welfare, and mailed Robby's mother a picture of the car (FT, 2026). Robby drives a Porsche 911 today: *"When I look into the rear-view mirror, I see his eyes in my eyes. It helps me have a relationship with him, enjoying something he enjoys. It's not ideal, but it's not nothing."*
- **On her queerness** (Anniewood, Jul 2024): *"It's promiscuous behavior not befitting a daughter of Israel."*
- **Estrangement:** Robby **has not spoken to her father since her early 20s**; he had already been mostly absent for years (Guardian, May 2026). *"I have no relationship with my father… He's somewhere in Nevada"* (RIP Jordan Jensen, Mar 2026). *"My father, who I don't speak to, so I can't ask him this"* (Here To Make Friends, Sep 2026). One of her sisters had recently visited him as of July 2024 (Anniewood). Robby still claims the resemblance: *"I definitely feel a lot like him."*

> [!note] Correction (2026-09-24)
> Earlier versions of this page and the [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby Hoffman]] page said her father had died, 15–20 years after the divorce. That came from an unlabeled line in the [[projects/blocks|Blocks]] transcript, where Robby and host [[people/neal-brennan|Neal Brennan]], also one of ten kids, compare families. In context it was most likely Neal describing his own father. Robby's own 2026 statements above describe her father as living.

## Siblings

Robby has **nine siblings: six older and three younger.** Her mother had all ten by age 30 over roughly 12.5 years, and Robby says they are *"all about 13 months apart"* (Made It Out, Jul 2024; Bein' Ian, Oct 2024). Most of them moved out at 17, as Robby did. Names below are as they appear in interviews; many come from audio transcripts, so spellings vary.

- **[[people/kaya-hoffman|Kaya]]:** the oldest sister. Lives in Scottsdale, Arizona with her husband Bruno. She attended the 2025 Emmys with Robby and walked her out after the loss. Like Robby, she is a 15-minutes-early person: *"Would you be late if the queen invited you for dinner? No. Well, you don't even know the queen. I'm your sister. You're flesh and blood."* Possibly the same sister called **Chaya** or **Khaya** in some retellings.
- **[[people/schmuly-hoffman|Schmuly]]:** older brother, a mall security guard in Canada (Saskatchewan, per Live From Bed, Aug 2025). He calls Gabby "toots." His biggest excitement about Robby's TV career was [[people/steve-carell|Steve Carell]] and [[people/tim-dylan|Tim Dylan]]. He lobbied Robby and Gabby to get a German Shepherd for LA: *"You live in LA, it's a dangerous place. You got to get the dog."* They have a Labradoodle, which Robby calls *"useless."*
- **Menachem (also transcribed "Manakim"):** brother; Robby borrows his pants. He was flagged underweight at the family's annual checkup (see [Growing up](#growing-up)).
- **"Shanaer"** (phonetic; exact name unclear): a brother in a corporate office who, in March 2023, complained to Robby about a Pride-month bulletin at work: *"The gay agenda is alive and well at work."* Robby: *"You know it's me. I'm a gay person."* His reply: *"Well, not you, you're my flesh and blood."*
- **Other brothers mentioned:** a color-blind brother who works at FedEx; and, in the GQ interview with John Mulaney (Dec 2025), a NyQuil brother, an eBay-flipping brother and an apple-a-day brother, Shmuel (possibly Schmuly).
- **D'vorah / Devorah:** a younger sister, "a meddler." She recently graduated, and Robby threw a large graduation event for her (Endless Honeymoon, Oct 2025). She appears in the bathroom photo in the FT profile.
- **"Debs":** the sister who drove a whiteout mountain route in a Nissan Versa to bring a spare key on Robby's first night at [[career/country-house|the country house]] (Here To Make Friends). Robby's reaction was guilt, not relief: *"I sacrificed my sister for the car."* Possibly D'vorah, but the source never connects them.
- **[[people/yehudis-hoffman|Yehudis]]:** a younger sister, close to Uncle Eddie. She **now works as Robby's assistant** and books the cheapest flights available: *"we're still in that mentality"* (We Got Company, Sep 2026).
- **A younger sister's late walk home** (NPR Newsmakers, Sep 2026): after closing a Starbucks at midnight, the sister got through *"a really sketchy few blocks"* by imitating a physical disability or carrying an umbrella rain or shine to look unwell. Robby turned it into a bit and later retired it, the only bit she has said on record that she stopped doing: *"It's offensive and it also sucks to be poor."* See [[projects/newsmakers]].
- **Robby's advice to a 19-year-old sister** at her wits' end (We Got Company): *"just 'cause you don't have a kid doesn't mean you don't have a dependent. You have a 19-year-old kid that you have to keep fed, educated, fulfilled. It is a thankless job."*

## Does Robby Hoffman have kids?

No. Robby has said on record that she isn't having children: *"I'm not having kids. This is what I do"* ([[projects/south-beach-sessions|South Beach Sessions]], May 2026). Growing up in the Orthodox community she assumed she'd marry and have *"six or seven kids"*; now, *"This life is way better"* (Tea Time, Jun 2025). She is an aunt: her nieces include **Teddy**, named after [[people/uncle-eddie|Uncle Eddie]], and the six-year-old daughter of the brother her mother lives with.

## Extended family

- **Grandfather ("Zadie") [[people/nathan-letovsky|Nathan Letovsky]]:** Canadian painter and post-war butcher who engineered the family's escape from Florida. His paintings hung "wherever there was a square inch spare" on his visits, and they were the only objects Robby grabbed from her house during the LA-fire evacuation.
- **Grandmother Lil:** mentioned on Good For You (EP 240).
- **[[people/uncle-eddie|Great-uncle Eddie]]:** took in the mother and ten kids in Montreal at a moment's notice. The family folk hero.
- **[[people/uncle-rob|Uncle Rob]]:** in Vermont; the uncle whose name Robby took professionally. See [[people/uncle-rob]].
- **Niece Teddy:** named after Uncle Eddie.

## Growing up

- **The escape:** [[career/montreal-flight|Grandfather Zadie and his French-Canadian best friend Germaine arrived in Florida with two cabs]], flew the family to Montreal in February, and [[people/uncle-eddie|great-uncle Eddie]] picked them up. The temporary stay became permanent.
- **Scale of the household:** one bathroom for twelve people, plus a pest infestation (Blocks, Feb 2026). Robby was the *"first girl in the family to have a bat mitzvah"* (Bless These Braces, Mar 2024).
- **The school interview:** see [[bits/package-deal|"It's a package deal"]]. Three brothers got into a fistfight during the interview; the school offered Robby the scholarship alone, and the grandfather refused. The boys lasted a year before being expelled. Robby graduated.
- **The annual checkup** (RIP Jordan Jensen, Mar 2026): once a year, before camp or school, the mother brought several kids to the doctor at once and all were weighed in their underwear. When Menachem was flagged underweight, the mother, humiliated, said: *"Get dressed, get dressed. You don't weigh enough, embarrassing me, like we don't have food."*
- **The art program** (Endless Honeymoon, Oct 2025): around age 11, Robby was gifted at drawing, and a teacher suggested a $45/year after-school art program. The mother's reply: *"We're fine with the art program just the way it is, thank you."* Robby now reads it as accidentally correct parenting: boredom and lack of curation taught her to keep herself company creatively.
- **Big Brother / Big Sister program, ended early** (Anniewood, Jul 2024): Robby had a Big Sister, **Sherry**, whom she loved. When one brother's Big Brother suggested an overnight camping trip, the mother ended the program for the whole family: *"He's not going anywhere. You'll grow up without a father from here on out."*
- **Nobody watched her perform** (We Got Company): a single mother of ten didn't make recitals, plays or parent-teacher nights, and at one point a teacher demanded proof a parent existed. *"I'm like, unless I'm failing, she's not coming."* When her mother finally came and heard there was no problem: *"How do you drag me to the school and there's no problem?"* The consequence for Robby: *"I got very comfortable not being watched… 10,000 people I don't know, I take that any day over you and my sister just there."*
- **Moved out at 17**, as most of the siblings did (We Got Company).

## Family today

- **A mutual-aid network, not a one-way rescue:** *"A lot of my family is successful. It's not just me… my older siblings did this too. Now I feel like I'm rising up and it's time for me to step up. We all step up when we can."* On [[projects/we-got-company|We Got Company]], siblings in sales are told to use her name: *"why can't I nepo my family if I can?"*
- **Family events are voluntary:** Kaya didn't come to D'vorah's graduation, and Robby's stance is *"wherever they can meet us, we're just thrilled."*
- **Finding out about her career** happened one project at a time; for Schmuly, the highlights were Steve Carell and Tim Dylan.
- **At home in LA:** Robby and Gabby host her brother, and Gabby's mother helps with the house.

## Why it matters

- Robby's comedy is constantly running the family count: number of kids, number of bathrooms, number of relatives still calling her Rifka.
- The family is split between two affective registers: the Montreal-Orthodox cluster (mother, brothers, Uncle Eddie) and the Robby/Kaya migration outward to LA, Arizona and New York.
