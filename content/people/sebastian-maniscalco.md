# Sebastian Maniscalco

**Sebastian Maniscalco** is a stand-up comedian and the host of the podcast [[projects/we-got-company|We Got Company]]. He is unusual in this wiki: an interviewer [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] openly reveres, and the only host on record who rattles her.

## Key Takeaways

- **He makes her nervous, and she says so repeatedly.** *"This is the most nervous I've ever been for any podcast and I barely get nervous… I'm pretending that I'm just talking, but really I'm not. I'm doing a very good job acting."*
- **She'd rather he not be in the room at all.** *"If you came to me behind stage, I'd be like — Sebastian's — I would be very nervous and it would be probably one of my worst shows."*
- **He found her through [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]].** He says he didn't know who she was until the Netflix special, watched it in bed with his wife, and — against his own rule of giving unproven comedians five minutes — watched the whole thing. *"I wasn't analyzing it. I was just, oh my god, this is so funny."*
- **His read on her craft** is about delivery: the inflections, the ups and downs, the physicality.
- **He is a model for the slow career.** Robby discovered him eight or nine years into her own run and took the timeline as permission: *"he hasn't been the overnight success either. People go, where's this guy coming from? I said, he's been out."*

## The jacket story

Robby's account of meeting him at the **Comedy Store**, told to his face. She had by then given up telling comedians she was a fan — *"when you tell people you're a fan of theirs, especially a lot of people with egos… they actually like you less. It comes desperate."* She broke the rule only because he spoke first, saying *"nice jacket"* about the tan suede Top Man jacket she'd just bought.

The story's real subject is what happened next. Another comedian interrupted the conversation to announce that he, too, had played Toronto; Maniscalco simply ended it — *"you just looked at him, gave him his 'was really nice meeting you.'"* Robby's framing: *"these are the stories that don't get out. If you're not hearing bad stories about someone, imagine the good stories — we don't share those."*

She also botched a later encounter at the **People's Choice Awards**, misreading Kylie Jenner as looking at her. *"I do embarrassing things all of the time."*

## Family connection

Robby's sister [[people/kaya-hoffman|Kaya]] and Kaya's French husband **Bruno** are Maniscalco fans — they made shirts, bought VIP tickets, and his show was the first stand-up Bruno ever saw.
