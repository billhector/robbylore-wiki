# Uncle Rob

**Uncle Rob** is the relative [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby Hoffman]] named herself after. He lived in **Vermont**, and Robby's stated reason for choosing him is a character reference: *"Nobody ever said anything bad about my Uncle Rob."*

## Key Takeaways

- **He is the origin of the stage name.** Robby was born with her Jewish name (**Rivkah Sarah**) and needed something else when she started comedy. She took his.
- **The reason was reputational, not aesthetic:** *"Nobody ever said anything bad about my Uncle Rob. Lived in Vermont. We didn't, you know, nice guy."*
- **The name was initially a disguise.** She was still working at the accounting firm, and stand-up was a fireable secret — so "Robby" was cover. It failed: a small write-up about her in a Montreal paper got back to the firm. *"Is this you?"* See [[career/accounting-to-standup]].
- **Distinct from [[people/uncle-eddie|Uncle Eddie]]**, the great-uncle whose Montreal roof the family lived under.

> [!warning] Contradiction
> [[people/robby-hoffman]] currently states that *"Robby"* is *"the diminutive that stuck once she started writing professionally."* Robby's own account here is different and more specific: it is **Uncle Rob's name, adopted deliberately as cover for the accounting job**, before the writing career. The 2026 first-person account should be treated as superseding the earlier inference.

## What we don't know

Almost everything. The source gives a first name, a state, and a character reference. No surname, no relation-degree (uncle versus great-uncle), no indication of whether he is living, and no other appearance across the wiki's sources to date.
