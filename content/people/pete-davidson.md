# Pete Davidson

Comedian and former *Saturday Night Live* cast member. [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] brings him up in two ways: as the comic whose style is the reverse of hers, and as the person who gave her a lesson in mixing family with show business.

## Key Takeaways

- **Her stylistic opposite.** On [[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]] she says she loves watching comics who are her *"antithesis,"* and names Davidson. He walks out of his special puffing a joint, *"so relaxed,"* *"not a zinger guy."* She loved his hour. She places herself in the *"Taylor Swift camp"*: works hard, prolific, tries hard. *"You can be both. You just have to know who you are."*
- **The Schmuly incident.** When Robby and Davidson did a show together, her brother [[people/schmuly-hoffman|Schmuly]] came backstage. She'd told him to *"be cool, be cool, be cool."* He went straight to Davidson with a photo: *"Look, it's me… that's a machine I drive everywhere."* (RIP Jordan Jensen Ep. 75.)
- **What she took from it.** She used to keep family away from industry rooms in case one bad impression cost her bookings. Now: *"Is it better for Pete Davidson to think I'm cool or to give my brother Schmuly the time of his life?"* She's confident enough in what she's built to take the hit.
- **He wants her on his podcast** as of October 2026 — *"thank god."*
