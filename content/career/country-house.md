# The Country House

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] **rents her apartment and owns a country house**, in that order and on purpose: *"you rent in the city and you buy out."* It is the first property she describes owning, and she is explicit that it took years of saving — *"I was saving for, saving for, saving for, and I did it."*

> [!note] Same property as the "writer's cabin"
> This is almost certainly the **little house / writer's cabin in the mountains** already recorded on [[people/robby-hoffman]] and [[people/gabby-windey]], dated to **2023** via *Interview* (Feb 2025) — the mountains, the isolation and the first-owned-property framing all match. The Here To Make Friends telling (Sep 2026) is the first account of **buying** it and of the first night there. Treated here as one property under two names unless a later source separates them.

## Key Takeaways

- **The apartment is a rental.** She calls it *"the nicest apartment, my dream apartment. We hope to be there, God willing, for as long as we can."* Independently confirmed on NPR, where a landlord calls mid-interview about a blocked line.
- **The house is the hedge, not the flex.** Her reasoning: buy something even if she can't live in it yet — *"worst case I go there."*
- **She had never owned anything.** Part of what made the first night strange was the building-versus-house distinction: no shared door between her and outside. *"A house is like — oh, I'm like, we're outside."*
- **The snowstorm rescue** is the story she gives as her "ravioli moment" — the time a friend showed up. It is about her sister.

## The first night

Driving up for the first time, she hit a snowstorm she hadn't expected that far south — *"snow like from Montreal."* The car broke down and the key didn't work. Her little sister **Debs**, whose Nissan Versa still had the all-wheel tires from a drive down from Vancouver, got the spare key and insisted on bringing it.

Robby's landlord only told her *after* Debs had left that nobody should be driving up. Debs lost phone service in the mountains. Robby had told her before she left: *"I don't care how slow you're going… It's not a crime to go slow. They can pass you."* The hour-and-a-half drive took two hours. Robby walked out into the dark with no power to watch the road, and eventually saw *"two little lights coming."*

*"I was like religious again… you make every deal with God."*

They fixed the car, went to the grocery store, and Robby insisted on the most expensive bottle in the store to mark the night. When Debs said *"we own a home,"* Robby's answer was *"it's yours, too."*

> [!note] Naming
> The sister is only ever called **"Debs"** in the source. She is plausibly the sister recorded elsewhere as **Devorah / D'vorah** (Devorah → Deborah → Debs), but no source connects them, so that's inference and she has no page of her own yet. See [[people/robby-hoffman]] and [[themes/family]].
