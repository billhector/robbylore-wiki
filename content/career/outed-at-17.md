# Outed at 17

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] was **outed at seventeen** and has said repeatedly that she never came out: *"I realized when Gabby came out, my wife came out. I never came out. I was outed."* The episode sits underneath most of her [[themes/queerness|queerness]] material and much of her relationship to disclosure generally.

## Key Takeaways

- **The mechanism:** a classmate saw the *end* of an embrace with her girlfriend in the bathroom of the student bar, told one person, and it *"spread like wildfire."*
- **The cost was real but temporary.** She lost her social circle *"overnight"* — for a few months. Two friends stayed solid throughout; the rest came back. She is still close with them, and is careful to say so on the record.
- **Part of it wasn't homophobia.** Her read on the friends who pulled away: some of it was jealousy about a new girlfriend pulling her out of the friend group.
- **The "you didn't tell us" complaint doesn't survive contact.** Robby: *"First of all, I didn't even tell myself. Second of all, I'm allowed not to tell you things."*
- **Two rhyming poems.** Her mother's approach to asking whether Robby was gay was to write her **two poems that rhymed**. Her mother then told her father.
- **The psychiatrist, on Robby's terms.** Her father made her go. Robby's condition: *"I won't go unless you come with me."* The therapist's verdict — Robby didn't need therapy, but recommended the parents stay on.
- **She wasn't resisting help.** Looking back: *"I would have been like, you know what, I did, thank you Ma, I agree, I don't want this either. Let's figure this out."*
- **The counterfactual.** Asked when she would have been ready: *"I don't know if I would have come out really. I probably just would have got gay enough that you knew."* The comparison offered on the podcast — someone who never announces a pregnancy and just *"gets pregnant enough."*
- **Timing made it worse.** Early 1990s, conservative religious environment: *"every year at that time… every year is like a decade of that."*
- **People knew first.** *"People said I'm gay before I said I'm gay. 'Cause I didn't want to be."*

## Details

Before the outing, Robby had boyfriends — including, she notes, a male model in high school. *"Guys liked me."* The self-monitoring she describes elsewhere (sitting "more girly" to keep a boyfriend in play) belongs to the same years; see [[projects/the-political-scene]].

The outing also landed inside a religious community, which is a separate axis of the story covered in the *Made It Out* interview and on [[themes/orthodox-upbringing]].
