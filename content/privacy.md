# Privacy

robbylore.org is a fan-run encyclopedia. This page explains what the site collects when you visit it.

## Key Takeaways

- The site uses **Google Analytics 4** to count visits and see which pages people read.
- There are no accounts, no comments, no ads, and no newsletter. The site never asks for your name or email.
- No data is sold or shared for advertising.
- You can block analytics with a content blocker or Google's opt-out add-on, and the site works the same.

## What's collected

**Google Analytics 4** sets first-party cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) and records:

- pages viewed and how you got here (referring site or search)
- approximate location (country/city level, derived from your IP address; GA4 doesn't store IP addresses)
- device type, browser, and screen size
- scrolls, outbound link clicks, and time on page

This data is only viewed in aggregate, to see which pages are useful and what to write next. It's processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Cloudflare** hosts and serves the site. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Opting out

- Install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- Or use any content or tracker blocker, or block cookies for this site.
