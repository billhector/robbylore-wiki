# Zero Personality Disorder

A bit from [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s first special, still in active use as her explanation for why she does nothing.

## Key Takeaways

- The diagnosis targets **nine-to-fivers who live for the weekend** and fill it with activities.
- *"What do I do on the weekend? Nothing. 'Cause I got a personality."*
- The logic: hobbies are compensation. *"I don't need to be making up for a lack of personality with this activity and this friend group and this book club."*

## The bit

Named on her first special, [[projects/i-m-nervous|I'm Nervous]]. The setup is the weekend hobbyist — rock climbing, activities, the book club — and the punch is that the activity is a prosthetic. *"I could sit there and do nothing for a minute."*

Asked directly on [[projects/we-got-company|We Got Company]] what she does to relax, she answers in the bit's own terms: *"I do nothing. I love doing nothing. Just at the house. Nothing."* What she actually does is YouTube — car-repair videos and philosophy — and on the road, a hotel all day with Judge Judy and QVC.

## Why it works

It inverts the usual shape of the question. The interviewer expects a hobby answer and gets a diagnosis of the question instead. It also does quiet class work: the weekend-as-compensation frame assumes a job you're escaping, which is exactly the life she describes escaping in [[themes/money]] — *"every day I don't wake up with the alarm and have to take the subway to work is a miracle."*
