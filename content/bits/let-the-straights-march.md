# Let the Straights March

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s explanation for why she doesn't watch other comedians' specials, from [[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]] (Sep 2026). It's one of her anti-Pride positions (see [[themes/queerness]]) applied to stand-up.

## Key Takeaways

- **The confession:** *"Hot take. I don't watch standup."* She's seen her peers' clips and watches documentaries instead.
- **The reasoning:** she's in comedy clubs all the time already. *"We are already supportive enough of standup. Let other people support it."*
- **The analogy:** *"It's like gay people marching. Why do I have to march in the sun? I'm already fucking gay. Let the straights march for us. You support us."* Then: *"All day gay."*
- **The trade version:** *"Carpenters don't go to other carpentry sites and go, 'Good job.'"*
- **The early-career version:** she resented having to hang out with club bookers to get spots. *"I have friends… Why are you making me be friends with you? I would never make somebody be friends with me."*

## Why it works

It takes the expectation that a member of a community should keep showing up for it, and turns it around: being in the community is the contribution, and the visible support is everyone else's job. It fits her stated discomfort with Pride (*"I find the whole thing cringe"*) without saying anything against gay people or comics.
