# The Ring Pop

Thirteen days into dating [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby Windey]], [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] bought a **ring pop** and asked her to be her girlfriend. Distinct from the later marriage proposal — see [[bits/proposing-via-crossword]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Thirteen days.** *"Gabby 13 days in I bought a ring pop and asked her with a ring pop she'd be my girlfriend."*
- **Robby classifies the move by orientation:** *"that is lesbian"* — and admits it's out of character. *"I've never moved in quick. I've never done any of that stuff."*
- **The summary line:** *"Gabby is the gayest I've ever been."*
- **"I love you" ran on the same clock** — she thinks she said it around day thirteen too.
- **Gabby said it once and then stopped.** Not a retreat, per Robby — she just wouldn't say it again. *"She got scared."*
- **Robby escalated in legal register:** *"I got a right to know."* She concedes the flaw immediately: *"I don't have a right to know. It's not really a right."*

## Why it works

The bit is the gap between Robby's stated temperament — slow, skeptical, never in a hurry — and the speed of this one relationship. She doesn't defend the inconsistency; she files it under being gay, which is funnier and also how she talks about the whole marriage in [[themes/marriage]].

The ring pop itself does the class work the wedding material does elsewhere: the gesture is enormous and the object costs a dollar.
