# Office Hours

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s pet peeve from university: students who stop the lecture to ask questions. *"Hello, there's office hours."* On [[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]] it turned into a running callback, and she applies the same rule to anyone who talks during her set.

## Key Takeaways

- **The origin.** At university she walked in on the first day of each class, grabbed the syllabus and left — *"I'm not doing intros… I can go over the syllabus."* People who asked questions mid-lesson drove her crazy: *"Can we finish the lesson? Don't ask midway."*
- **The proposal.** Hold questions for the last few minutes, let everyone who doesn't have one leave, then gather around the professor's desk. Better still: *"Please utilize the office hours."*
- **The follow-up is worse.** *"Bro, office hours. There's 30 other people here."*
- **The stage version.** Robby doesn't get many hecklers — *"I don't come off friendly and I don't come off like I want to be interacted with."* When someone does call out, even something nice, she treats it the same way. Pete Holmes: *"You go office hours."* Robby: *"Literally office hours. Not during the lecture."*
- **Even the phone.** When she ends a call abruptly at the end of the taping, Holmes calls the move *"so office hours."*

## Why it works

It's the scarcity logic of [[themes/money|a poor kid working 30 hours a week]] applied to time. The lecture is something she's paying for, everyone else's questions eat into it, and the polite procedure already exists. It's also how she runs a room: she holds court, and there's a proper time for the audience to talk.
