# Born Jewish and Poor

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] riffing on [[projects/rip-jordan-jensen|RIP Jordan Jensen]] Ep. 75 (Oct 2026) about how close her family came to being comfortable.

## Key Takeaways

- **The setup:** *"You're born Jewish in New York. You think you hit the jackpot."*
- **The fumble:** *"How do you even get born Jewish and poor? It's insane… How did my mother fumble that?"*
- **The near miss:** *"We were so close."* (Brooklyn, technically — the exchange notes it wasn't Manhattan — *"but you know what I mean."*)
- **The kid's-eye diagnosis:** growing up she saw the city around her and felt *"something went really wrong."* She liked herself; everything else — roaches, a freezing house — was the problem.

## Why it works

It plays on the stereotype that Jews are rich and makes her own family the exception. Robby's poverty shows up everywhere in her material ([[themes/money]]); this version blames the family's bad luck at birth rather than anything they did.
