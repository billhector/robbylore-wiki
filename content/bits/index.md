# Bits

Recurring jokes, signature bits, catchphrases, and segments that Robby revisits across appearances. Material she does *once* lives in episode/project pages; material she keeps doing earns its own bit page.

Grouped by theme below. The clusters map onto [[themes/index|Themes]] — and the sizes are themselves informative: roughly a third of everything she does is a money bit.

## Money + class

The largest cluster, and the engine of most of the others. Nearly all of these convert deprivation into competence rather than asking for sympathy.

- [[bits/cost-per-wear|Cost-per-Wear]] — daily Rolex amortization; "a dollar a day," and falling.
- [[bits/crispy-towels|Crispy Towels]] — the towel that exfoliates; you don't inherit a towel.
- [[bits/eggs-on-the-bus|Boiled Eggs on the Bus]] — sustenance, and a guaranteed empty seat.
- [[bits/girls-need-gifts|Girls Need Gifts]] — the charity donation in your name is a scam; bring back cash in a card.
- [[bits/bring-nothing|Bring Nothing]] — the hosting doctrine, and why the potluck was a lesbian mistake.
- [[bits/lil-rob|Lil Rob]] — reporting back to the poor kid she was on how the extraction is going.
- [[bits/polo-lounge|The Polo Lounge $7 Beer]] — the early-LA ritual: one beer, in the room where it happens.
- [[bits/supercuts-tuesday|Supercuts Tuesday]] — the discount-day haircut, still observed.

- [[bits/multiples-of-18|Multiples of 18]] — gematria for *chai*, life; she still negotiates in 18s.
