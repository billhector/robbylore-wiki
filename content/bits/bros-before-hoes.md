# "Bros before hoes"

The name [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] gives one of her jokes about [[themes/orthodox-upbringing|fundamentalist religion]] and the position of women within it.

## Key Takeaways

- Robby's argument, set up on [[projects/working-it-out|Working It Out]]: *"Most fundamentalist sects of any religion originate at times when women weren't [equal]."* Birbiglia: *"They're bros before hoes."* Robby: *"That's how they would categorize it — 'bros before hoes' is one of the names of my joke."*
- Folds into her broader [[themes/orthodox-upbringing|Lubavitch-upbringing]] material — *"my parents had ten kids by 30… I think it's extremely reckless,"* and *"it can be very oppressive, particularly of women."*
- Consistent with the [[themes/queerness|queerness]] throughline: religion as a system that subordinates women and queer people, observed from inside.
