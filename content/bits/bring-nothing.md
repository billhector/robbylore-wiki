# Bring Nothing

[[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s hosting rule, and her standing objection to the potluck.

## Key Takeaways

- **The instruction she gives guests:** *"Bring nothing."*
- **The potluck is a cultural error she names by community:** *"the potluck of it all — that's very lesbian and we started a bad thing. I don't do it."* See [[themes/queerness]].
- **As a guest she reciprocates the doctrine:** *"if you ever invite me to a soirée, I'm bringing nothing."*
- **What "bring nothing" actually signals** is that the host has it covered: *"not only am I bringing nothing, I know I will be taken care of… There's going to be something to eat. There's going to be something to drink. There's going to be shade. There's going to be sun. We are handled."*
- **It scales with means, and she says so.** Earlier, broke: *"I would message a friend — could you bring this? I have wine, can you grab the beer?"* Now: *"bring nothing. So it depends where we're at."*
- **The exception is a birthday.** Gifts are still owed to the person. See [[bits/girls-need-gifts]].

## Why it works

Most hosting talk is about generosity; Robby's is about **capacity**, which makes it a money bit. "Bring nothing" is a boast you can only make once you can afford it, and she's explicit that she couldn't always. The [[projects/wake-up|Wake Up]] watch party is the worked example — no premiere, a private bartender in her living room, *"I said bring nothing"* — and the punchline is that the bartender she'd assumed would cost $10,000 cost $400.
