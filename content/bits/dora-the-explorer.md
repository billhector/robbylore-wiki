# "Dora the Explorer"

The opening line [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] used on [[people/gabby-windey|Gabby]] when they met. Gabby — fresh off [[projects/the-bachelorette|The Bachelorette]], age 28 — described herself as "exploring" her sexuality. Robby's reply: *"Exploring? What are you, Dora the Explorer?"*

## Key Takeaways

- Canonical Robby + Gabby origin-story line — quoted across multiple interviews.
- Sets the tone of their dynamic: Robby cutting Gabby's earnestness with a sharp comic deflate.
- **The 2026 retelling** ([[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]], Sep 2026) makes the line a callback. Earlier in the same interview Robby explains that as a kid she was told 13 was the age for "exploring." So when Gabby says she's exploring: *"How old are you? What are you, Dora the Explorer?… Exploring is when you're 13. We've been through this."* This version gives Gabby's age as 30 (other tellings say 28 or 32; see [[people/gabby-windey]]). The rest of the approach: both were waiting for Ubers, Robby had *"maybe a minute,"* and she offered dinner after a one-week tour — *"I'll wear a mask. I make sure I'm not sick."* Someone told her Gabby was the Bachelorette: *"The Bachelorette's gay?"*
