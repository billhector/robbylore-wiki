# Megans, Pick a Spelling

A new premise [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] tried out on [[projects/you-made-it-weird|You Made It Weird]] (Sep 2026) when Pete Holmes asked to hear something she was working on. She called it *"just the nugget of it."*

## Key Takeaways

- **The premise:** Megans need to *"get it together and figure out how to spell their name."* Meghan Markle, Megyn Kelly — *"enough is enough."*
- **The process she suggests:** *"Take a poll. Do whatever it is. Nobody comes out of the jury room without a decision."*
- **Already growing:** Julie and Julia are *"the same name. It's enough."* Holmes adds that it's a problem from the 1100s, when people had more time.
- **How she builds bits:** she writes the idea down, takes it on stage, and *"something will come of that"* — but the first version is mostly how it stays. *"It's like a one-two punch."*

## Why it works

It's a small grievance — her own term for her style, see [[people/robby-hoffman]] — delivered as civic policy. The jury-room line turns a spelling quirk into a group's failure to do its job.
