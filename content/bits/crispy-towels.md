# Crispy Towels

One of [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]]'s standing poverty bits: the household towel that had gone **crispy**.

## Key Takeaways

- The towels were so far gone they exfoliated — *"the one towel that exfoliates."*
- The real punch is the ignorance, not the poverty: she didn't know that **you don't inherit a towel**.
- The bit is well-known enough that hosts bring it up to her unprompted.

## The follow-through

Pressed on whether a new towel fixes it, Robby says it doesn't — and this is the part that makes it a bit rather than an anecdote. A new towel *"absorbs nothing,"* so the crispy towel wasn't worse at the job, it was better: *"you revisit that towel in two to three years. It's cutting, and you put it in every wash."*

## Why it works

It follows the same pattern as [[bits/eggs-on-the-bus]] — the deprived object turns out to be the *superior* object, so the audience's pity gets converted into an argument. She never asks to be felt sorry for; she argues the new towel is the defective one.

Sits alongside the rest of the household-scarcity material in [[themes/money]] and [[themes/family]] — one bathroom, no private room, hand-me-downs across ten kids.
