# "AI is autistic"

A new [[people/robby-hoffman|Robby]] premise from her 2026 theater tour, workshopped on tape on [[projects/working-it-out|Mike Birbiglia's Working It Out]].

## Key Takeaways

- The premise: *"I think AI is autistic. The vibe is off."* — sometimes charming, sometimes you go *"are you okay?"*
- The mechanism: *"Who's inventing her? We got guys like Elon Musk and Sam Altman — the most autistic people on the planet who are at the base of her. So now all of our AI is going to be autistic."*
- Robby's framing is **claim-not-insult**: she's been *"accused myself,"* considers being on the spectrum *"my territory until proven otherwise,"* and argues *"autistic people like autistic comedy."* See [[themes/queerness]] / neurotype on [[people/robby-hoffman]].
- Ties to a recurring Robby thesis about authorship and representation — *"who wrote the actual books of history?"* — whoever isn't part of building the system isn't reflected in it.
- Pairs in her set with the they/them stance (*"it's annoying and we'll do it"*) and the [[bits/bros-before-hoes|bros before hoes]] joke as examples of jokes she'll get emails about — and ignore.
